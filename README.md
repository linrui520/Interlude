Interlude is a native Mac app built with Swift and SwiftUI, bringing focus timers, to-do lists, and music together in a minimal interface with fluid animations.

It supports automatic focus and break cycles, task-linked time tracking, daily goals, and custom presets. Play your own music, mix synthesized rain and ocean soundscapes, and reflect on your progress through a focus calendar and weekly reports.

With English and Simplified Chinese interfaces, customizable themes, menu bar controls, a floating player, local backups, and a full reset option, Interlude fits into your daily routine. No account is required, your data stays on your device, and the source code is available under the MIT License.

间奏（Interlude）是一款使用 Swift 和 SwiftUI 开发的原生 Mac 专注工具，以简约界面和流畅动画，将专注计时、待办清单与音乐播放融为一体。

它支持自动切换专注与休息、任务关联计时、每日目标和自定义场景。你可以播放自己的音乐，叠加雨声、海浪等合成声景，并通过专注日历和每周报告回顾投入的时间。

间奏提供中英文界面、主题切换、菜单栏控制、悬浮播放器、本地备份与数据初始化。无需注册账户，数据保存在本机，源码采用 MIT 许可证开放。
